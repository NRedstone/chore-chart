# The Chore Chart — Quick Guide

## What it is
A shared chore and rewards tracker for the whole family. Kids check off their own chores; everyone earns points toward rewards you set up.

## The five chore types
- **Morning / Evening** — reset fresh each day, only on the days you've chosen (every day, weekdays, weekends, or a custom pick).
- **Anytime** — no fixed slot. Either a **one-time** task (stays checked through the rest of that day, then quietly clears) or a **weekly** one with a due day — shows "Due by [day]" until the deadline, then "Overdue" with a small red clock icon if it slips past.
- **Bonus** — extra, optional tasks worth their own points, paid the moment they're checked. Runs on its own day schedule, just like Morning/Evening.
- **Books** — one-time, worth their own points, and kept as a permanent "Books read" record even after they clear from the main list.

## Earning points
- **Any chore — Morning, Evening, or Anytime included — can carry its own point value**, set when adding or editing it. Leave it at 0 (the default) and it works exactly like before; give it a real value and it pays out the instant it's checked, just like Bonus and Books always have.
- Separately, finishing **every** Morning, Evening, and Anytime chore in a day still earns the day's completion bonus, on top of whatever individual chores paid — these two are independent, not either/or.
- Tap the coin icon next to any kid's name to see their full day-by-day history.

## Redeeming rewards
Tap **Redeem** next to a kid's points to spend them from the reward menu. Redemptions log automatically so you can see what's been claimed.

## Kids' own avatar
Tap your own avatar circle (top of your section) to pick a color and an emoji — no PIN needed, this one's just for fun.

## The lock (for parents)
Most of the app is open for kids to use freely — checking off chores, adding their own tasks. A few things need the **parent PIN**:
- Editing or deleting any chore
- Renaming kids, adjusting a kid's point total, and creating or managing the rewards menu
- Downloading a backup of everything, or restoring from one
- Managing linked devices: see below
- Adding a chore with a real point value while locked — you'll be offered a choice right there: enter the PIN to approve it immediately, or skip to submit it for approval instead

The Kids, Rewards, backup and Devices tools live under **Settings** (the gear button that appears once you unlock), in the tabs **Kids**, **Rewards**, **Screen saver**, **Household** and **Devices**.

Tap the padlock to unlock. It automatically re-locks after **2 minutes of inactivity** — not a flat timer, so it won't cut you off mid-task — or tap it again to lock immediately.

## Reordering & multiple kids
- **Drag chores** by the small handle (only visible unlocked) to reorder them within their category.
- When **adding** a new chore, you can select several kids at once to create it for all of them in one go.

## Signing in and linking devices
- **The first screen asks how you're getting started.** **Start a new chart** is for the parent who sets it up. **Join a family's chart** is for every other device. **Already the owner? Sign in** is for the owner on a new phone or computer.
- **The owner signs in with Google, or with an email and password.** An email account gets a confirmation email first; tap the link in it and the app moves on by itself. **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen sends a reset link.
- **Other devices, like the kids' tablet or the other parent's phone, don't need an email.** On the owner's phone: unlock, tap **Settings**, then **Devices**, then **Link a new device**. A code appears; it works once and expires in 10 minutes. On the other device, tap **Join a family's chart** and type it.
- In **Devices** the owner can rename or remove a device (it's shut out immediately), **sign out all other devices** (for a lost phone), or **transfer ownership** to someone else's account (Google or email). The last two ask the owner to sign in again (or type their password) to confirm it's them.
- **If you used the app before sign-in existed:** you'll see "Secure this household with a sign-in". Tap it and sign in. Your other devices keep working for 14 days while you link them with codes; **End it now** closes that window early.
- Joined devices can use everything in the app, including parent controls with the PIN, but only the owner can add or remove devices.

## Clock screen saver
Under **Settings, Screen saver** you can turn on a big clock that appears after 5, 10 or 30 minutes with no taps. It's set per device, so turn it on for a wall tablet and leave phones alone. Tap anywhere to come back. On an iPad, set Auto-Lock to Never so the screen stays on.

## If something looks stuck
If changes don't seem to be saving, a red banner will appear at the top. It usually resolves on its own — if it persists, try reloading the page.
